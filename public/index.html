A little about me

P G Ayush Rai — currently pursuing B.E. Computer Science & Engineering with AI/ML specialisation at VVCE, Mysuru. I build at the intersection of software, AI, and mission-critical systems.

Over the past year I've shipped production-grade computer-vision tools (posture detection, face recognition, line-following robotics), full-stack apps for campus & civic transit, and auto-tracking systems used in live events. Four major wins in 2025 — Teck Spark, Prayog, Anveshan, Pals Case Study Challenge.

Off-keyboard I serve as NCC Naval Cadet Captain and Vice President of VF, where systems thinking and field leadership meet weekly. I care about defense tech, civic engineering, and code that actually gets used.
