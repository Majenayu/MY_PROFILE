Hi, I'm Ayush — a builder at the crossroads of AI, defense tech, and civic good. Currently pursuing B.E. CSE-AIML at VVCE, I serve as NCC Naval Cadet Captain and Vice President (VF), where engineering and leadership meet every week.

I build real products that ship. Posture detection that coaches athletes, face recognition systems deployed in auditoriums, bus tracking that saves commuters hours, line-following robots that helped us win hackathons.
