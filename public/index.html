A little about me

I'm P G Ayush Rai — a fourth-year B.E. student specialising in AI & Machine Learning at Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering. I write production-grade code, train models that run on edge hardware, and lead teams when the stakes are nontrivial.

My day looks like equal parts computer-vision research, full-stack engineering with Node and React, and command-grade discipline as a Naval Cadet Captain. The throughline is the same — solve real problems for real people and ship the work.

In 2025 alone I won four flagship championships — Teck Spark, Prayog, Anveshan, and the Pals Case Study. Right now I'm building a campus-wide bus & asset tracking platform and a posture-correction robot. Both ship before graduation.
